About Me

My work sits at the intersection of software engineering, machine learning, and geospatial data. I've shipped a campaign finance transparency site used at a mayoral debate, rebuilt a 20-year-old flood advisory platform into a modern web app, trained classifiers on hundreds of thousands of Congressional speeches, and fused LiDAR, precipitation, and FEMA data into flood-risk models that outperform existing federal baselines.

I care most about software that holds up under real use — tools that communities, campaigns, and researchers can rely on, not just prototypes. Whether that means optimizing a model's accuracy or making a map load fast enough for someone deciding whether to evacuate, I build for the people on the other end.
